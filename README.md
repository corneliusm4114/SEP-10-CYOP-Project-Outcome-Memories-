# SEP-10-CYOP-Project-Outcome-Memories-

## What is Outcome Memories?

  _Outcome Memories is game on Roblox based on the famous creepypasta Sonic.Exe. Players must survive the Killers, known as Executioners using the Survivors. There are many maps in the game and also a well developed lore, so if you want to try the game yourself, feel free to!_

## Who are the executioners? 
_The executioners (also known as exes) are entities that were brought into this purgatory by the king of outcome memories who is known as King. These executioners are based on the creepypastas of the famous Sonic The Hedgehog, some were brought out of their own free will while some were brought in this world by force. These executioners' main goal is to kill the survivors and prevent them from escaping before the timer runs out.
